Gov. Abigail Spanberger announced in October that M.C. Dean will invest $412.5 million and create 1,950 jobs in Caroline County, expanding its Modular Mission Critical manufacturing campus.

## Two new facilities

The project adds a 442,000-square-foot building at the existing main campus and a 1.5-million-square-foot facility on the north campus, both using industrial machinery, automation and robotics. By 2030, the campus is expected to span more than 3.6 million square feet across 1,300 acres, which coverage describes as the largest modular manufacturing campus in the United States.

## State support

Virginia's Talent Accelerator Program will provide customized recruiting and training services. The state has approved up to $45 million in project support, subject to General Assembly approval. A Power Systems Training Center is planned at Caroline High School in 2027.

The Modular Mission Critical campus opened in 2023.
