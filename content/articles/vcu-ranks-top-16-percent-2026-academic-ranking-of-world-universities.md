Virginia Commonwealth University has been ranked among the top 16% of universities in the 2026 Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU), published by ShanghaiRanking Consultancy, which evaluates 2,500 institutions on research and academic indicators. VCU sits in the 301–400 band.

## Subject rankings

ShanghaiRanking's 2026 Global Ranking of Academic Subjects placed 10 VCU subjects, including:

- Education: 51–75
- Dentistry and oral sciences: 76–100
- Clinical medicine: 101–150
- Human biological sciences, pharmacy, and psychology: 151–200
- Public health, nursing, statistics and management: 201–300

## Global context

In June, U.S. News & World Report's 2026–27 Best Global Universities list put VCU in the top 20%, tied for No. 400 of 2,250 universities in 105 countries, with 16 subject areas ranked. Gastroenterology and hepatology ranked No. 8 of 250, and clinical medicine No. 145 of 1,250.

Hernan Bucheli, vice president for strategic enrollment management and global initiatives, said ShanghaiRanking "carries significant weight as a measure of research strength," particularly for students and families in East and Southeast Asia, where it can shape how institutions are assessed.
