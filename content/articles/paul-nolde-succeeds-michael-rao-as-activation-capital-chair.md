Activation Capital, the Richmond organization that supports life sciences entrepreneurs and startups, has a new board chair. VCU President Michael Rao, who has chaired the board since 2009, nominated Vice Chair Paul Nolde, and the board approved him.

## A planned handoff

Activation Capital is the operating name of the Virginia Biotechnology Research Partnership Authority. Rao succeeded Eugene Trani as chair in 2009 and had discussed a leadership transition since 2023. He stayed on after the 2024 departure of the former president and CEO, through an interim period and the first year of current President and CEO Michael Steele. He will remain an active board member.

Nolde joined the board in January 2025, has led meetings as vice chair, and has a background in business incubation, acceleration and venture capital. The board will fill its remaining officer posts, including vice chair, at its next regular meeting.

## What they said

"Paul brings outstanding economic development and entrepreneurial experience and commitment," Rao said. Nolde said Activation Capital has "a unique role in connecting entrepreneurs, researchers, investors and industry."

Rao noted VCU's goals of doubling sponsored research to $1 billion, launching a new strategic plan and advancing a $1.8 billion capital campaign.
