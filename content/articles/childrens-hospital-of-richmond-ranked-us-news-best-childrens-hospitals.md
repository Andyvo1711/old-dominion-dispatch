Children's Hospital of Richmond at VCU (CHoR) is again among the nation's best pediatric hospitals in the U.S. News & World Report 2026–2027 Best Children's Hospitals rankings.

## The rankings

CHoR ranked in these specialties:

- Nephrology: No. 24
- Urology: No. 35
- Pulmonology and lung surgery: No. 36
- Orthopaedics: No. 37
- Cancer: No. 40
- Pediatric and adolescent behavioral health: top 50

The hospital also ranks among the top 10 children's hospitals in the Mid-Atlantic and is the only Virginia hospital ranked among the best for pediatric cancer this year. It was the 12th national ranking for its nephrology program.

## Expansion ahead

CHoR President Elias Neujahr said the hospital will add 24 acute care beds and more outpatient space in the coming months. VCU President Michael Rao said CHoR "represents the best of our mission where research, clinical excellence and compassionate care come together."

U.S. News partnered with RTI International to analyze hospital-submitted data and survey input from thousands of pediatric experts. Only 50 hospitals can be recognized as best in each specialty.
