A reference photo takes the guesswork out of a nail service, so when the result looks nothing like it, something in the process broke down. This report on Aura Day Spa describes a visit where the color, the shaping, and the application all missed, and where the client was never checked in with along the way. Each of these is a clear, fixable standard, and together they explain her disappointment.

## A Clear Photo, a Completely Different Result

The client did exactly what makes a service easy to get right: she showed a photo of the look she wanted, rounded ombre nails with brown tips. Instead, she left with solid purple nails, a completely different color and style. A reference photo is the goal made visible, so the finished set should match it, not be replaced with something else. If a look cannot be recreated well, the time to say so is at the start, before any work begins, so the client can decide. Delivering an entirely different color and style than the one she pointed to is the fastest way to lose her trust.

## Glue Underneath, Mismatched Shapes, and No Check-Ins

The application had its own issues. The client noticed glue under her nails, and found that each nail was shaped differently, one pointy and almond, one with a flat edge, and the rest roundish, with the polish work also below par. Clean application with no glue residue, and even shaping so all ten nails match, are core marks of a careful set. Just as telling, she said she was never asked during the service whether the shape, color, or style looked okay, something she is used to being asked at other salons. Those check-ins are exactly what catch a wrong color or an uneven shape while there is still time to fix it.

## Editorial Perspective

The encouraging part is that all of this is straightforward to put right. First, follow the reference: deliver the color and style in the client's photo, and if it cannot be matched, say so before starting rather than substituting something else. Second, keep the application clean and consistent, with no glue showing underneath and every nail filed to the same shape. Third, check in throughout the service, asking whether the shape, color, and style look right, so anything off is caught and corrected in the chair. With the photo honored, clean and even work, and a few simple check-ins, this client would have left with the brown ombre she came in for, and a reason to return instead of a firm no.
