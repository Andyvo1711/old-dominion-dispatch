VCU Health and city and state leaders marked a construction milestone on Sept. 29 with a topping-off ceremony for the Pauley Heart Center Pavilion in Richmond's Diamond District.

## The building

The 93,000-square-foot outpatient pavilion is rising on a seven-acre site on Sherwood Avenue, near the Children's Hospital of Richmond Brook Road Pavilion and Interstates 64 and 95. It will house outpatient cardiac diagnostics and procedures, including MRI and CT imaging, plus lab and research space, classrooms and cardiac rehabilitation. The rehab space includes an indoor track that VCU Health describes as the first full-size cardiac rehab walking track in Virginia. The pavilion is expected to serve patients in 2028.

## The ceremony

Builder Kjellstrom & Lee hoisted the final beam, topped with a tree and an American flag in keeping with tradition. Shirley Gibson, Haley Felton and Jeannette Cain signed the beam beforehand.

"The need for accessible, complex cardiovascular care in Central Virginia has never been greater," said Marlon Levy, CEO of VCU Health. VCU President Michael Rao noted that cardiovascular disease remains the number one cause of death in America. Greg Hundley, director of the Pauley Heart Center, said the milestone "took years of planning, collaboration, and investment."

U.S. News & World Report ranks VCU Health's flagship medical center No. 29 nationally and No. 1 in Virginia for cardiology, heart surgery and vascular surgery.
