Reston-based technology contractor Data Machines is expanding its headquarters and plans to add 75 jobs, Gov. Abigail Spanberger and the Fairfax County Economic Development Authority announced Sept. 17.

## The expansion

The company will invest $849,000 to add about 10,000 square feet to its 6,500-square-foot headquarters at 12100 Sunset Hills Road, creating a dedicated innovation and engineering office focused on AI, cloud infrastructure, data analytics, cyber defense, software engineering, and modeling and simulation. Completion is targeted for March 2027, with hiring starting immediately. The Virginia Economic Development Partnership will provide recruiting and training support through the Jobs Investment Program.

## Leadership

Marine Corps veteran Eric Whyne founded Data Machines in 2016 and remains president and chief innovation officer; Matt Quinn is CEO. The company provides data science and IT services to federal agencies, including intelligence and national security customers.

Chief Operations Officer Catherine Schymanski said the company hopes to reach 75 hires without waiting for construction to finish. Fairfax County Board Chairman Jeff McKay called the move a strong vote of confidence in the county.

The governor's office says Spanberger has announced more than $5 billion in business investment and over 3,200 new jobs since taking office in January.
