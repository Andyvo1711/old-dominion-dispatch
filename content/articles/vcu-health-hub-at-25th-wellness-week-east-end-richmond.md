The VCU Health Hub at 25th, at 25th Street and Nine Mile Road in Richmond's East End, held its second annual Wellness Week in early October, bringing community groups together to help residents connect with resources.

## What happened

More than 24 organizations took part, including VCU, VCU Health, VCU Police, VCU Safety Ambassadors, the Medical Legal Partnership, the Small Business Opportunity Center and the Capital Area Health Network (CAHN). The week featured partner tabling, interactive sessions on financial literacy, maternal health and mental health and wellness, and bingo cards that encouraged residents to collect information from partners. CAHN, a 2026 Health Hub seed grant recipient, led a session on the link between oral health and overall health.

## Residents' reactions

"The Health Hub is a great resource where you can discover other organizations and services," resident Mille McCrae said. Operations manager Sarah McCall said the event supports belonging and connection. Community health worker Ivy Bell said it was "great to see attendees who didn't know that we exist," and noted that one couple who attended later joined an exercise class.

Access to resources and transportation have historically been the main challenges for East End residents seeking help, the article noted.
