Virginia Commonwealth University has named Candice Smith, an Emmy-winning anchor and reporter at WWBT (Channel 12), the Richmond NBC affiliate, as the featured speaker for its December commencement.

## From VCU student to Richmond anchor

Smith, a Roanoke native, earned a bachelor's degree in mass communications from VCU in 2014. While still a student she worked part time as an editor for WWBT's evening newscasts. She joined the station full time in 2015 as a digital content producer and social media reporter, filled in on morning and weekend traffic reports, and became the full-time morning traffic anchor in 2016.

Today she is the traffic anchor for the "12 News Today" morning program and hosts "12 About Town Today," a midday lifestyle show that highlights Richmond leaders, events and cultural happenings.

## Ceremony details

The universitywide commencement takes place Dec. 12 at 10 a.m. at the Stuart C. Siegel Center.
