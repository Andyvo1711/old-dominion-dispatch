The American Association of Colleges of Nursing (AACN) has given its 2026 Exemplary Academic-Practice Partnership Award to the VCU School of Nursing and the VCU Health System, recognizing an integrated model that links clinical care, academic inquiry and community health.

## What the award recognizes

The partnership was formalized in 2021 and is governed through quarterly leadership sessions and monthly faculty practice assemblies. Between 2021 and 2026, undergraduate nursing enrollment doubled to more than 650 students. Some 237 health system clinicians mentored 727 advanced practice students, who completed 77,890 clinical hours.

Other results cited include:

- A guaranteed admission pathway to the RN-to-BS program for VCU Health nurses with associate degrees, with application fees covered and tuition assistance offered
- A nurse residency program that enrolled 318 residents in 2025, with a one-year retention rate of 87.1%
- Doctor of Nursing Practice projects tied to health system priorities, growing from six in spring 2023 to a projected 55 through 2027
- A Mobile Health and Wellness Program delivering preventive and chronic disease care in eight rural and urban communities

Amber Balzer, who directs advanced practice professional development at VCU Health, called the award "a significant achievement." School of Nursing Dean Patricia Kinser credited trust between the school and the health system.
