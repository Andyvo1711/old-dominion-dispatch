Virginia's new restrictions on what SNAP benefits can buy take effect statewide on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, under a federal waiver intended to encourage healthier purchases.

## What changes

Regular, diet and zero-sugar soda, carbonated energy drinks, and other carbonated beverages with 5 grams or more of added sugar per serving will no longer be eligible. Lemonade, iced tea and non-carbonated sports drinks remain eligible even when they contain added sugar. Monthly benefit amounts are unchanged, recipients do not need new EBT cards, and restricted drinks can still be bought with cash, debit or other non-SNAP payment. Retailers must update their checkout systems.

## Experts weigh in

Jamie Zoellner, a University of Virginia professor of public health sciences, noted that a 12-ounce soda and 12 ounces of lemonade contain a similar amount of sugar, and said it "remains to be seen" whether the policy will measurably improve diets. Sonya Islam, a clinical dietitian at Children's Hospital of Richmond at VCU, advised families to check nutrition labels and consider unsweetened sparkling or flavored water, adding that not every purchase has to be nutritionally perfect.

Details are available in the Virginia Department of Social Services SNAP Healthy FAQ.
