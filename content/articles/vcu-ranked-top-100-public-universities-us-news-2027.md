Virginia Commonwealth University has been ranked among the nation's top 100 public universities in the U.S. News & World Report 2027 Best Colleges list, and was recognized in a new "best value" category for in-state students.

## Where VCU landed

VCU tied for No. 79 among best public universities and ranked No. 156 among national universities overall. In the new Best Value Schools category for in-state students, it placed No. 92, and it was No. 110 on the list of best colleges for veterans. VCU says it now ranks in the top 50 in 23 categories across graduate, online and undergraduate programs.

## Program highlights

- **Nursing (B.S.N.):** tied for No. 27, up 13 spots
- **Business:** tied for No. 116
- **Engineering:** tied for No. 102
- **Psychology:** tied for No. 103
- **Computer science:** tied for No. 141

## What leaders said

"This national recognition highlights the strength of the VCU learning experience and our dedication to student success," VCU President Michael Rao said. Provost Arturo P. Saavedra said rankings are useful proof points for the university's mission, although they do not capture everything the institution does. Patricia Kinser, dean of the School of Nursing, called the nursing climb "a powerful affirmation" of the school's momentum.

The university also pointed to earlier 2026 recognition: the School of the Arts ranked No. 2 among graduate programs in April, and VCU Medical Center was named No. 1 in Virginia in the Best Hospitals rankings in August.
