Several Virginia Republicans have asked Gov. Abigail Spanberger to call a special session to consider a 90-day pause on the state gas tax.

## The proposal

The request follows North Carolina's passage of a bill suspending its 41-cent tax on gasoline and diesel through Nov. 30. Virginia Republicans proposed a gas tax holiday in March, but it never took effect. House Minority Leader Terry Kilgore and other GOP lawmakers say a three-month freeze would save drivers up to about 32 cents per gallon. "Here is a way that we can reduce your families gas bill or your companies diesel bill and that's what this is about," Kilgore said.

## The cost

Republican projections put the state's revenue loss from a three-month freeze at more than $400 million, which they say the general fund can cover. The Virginia DMV says gas-tax revenue funds statewide road construction, bridge maintenance and public transportation.

## The governor's response

A spokesperson for Spanberger said the governor is focused on relief for families, is weighing all available options to address higher costs, and is working on long-term affordability solutions. The statement blamed higher prices on President Trump's war.
