Northern Virginia's housing market saw more homes for sale, fewer closings and higher prices in September, according to the Northern Virginia Association of Realtors® (NVAR), using Bright MLS data as of Oct. 12.

## Regional numbers (September 2026 vs. September 2025)

- Closed sales: 1,250, down 7.5%
- Sales volume: about $1.1 billion, down 8.7%
- Median sold price: $744,950, up 4.2%
- Average days on market: 28, unchanged
- Active listings: 3,477, up 30.7%
- Months of supply: 2.5, up 29.5%

Condo and townhome inventory jumped 49.7%, while detached-home inventory rose 5.7%.

## Loudoun County

Loudoun recorded 338 closed sales, down 17.8%, with a median sold price of $758,500, up 0.8%. Active listings rose 34.1% to 1,031, and average days on market increased 6.9% to 31.

## What NVAR says

NVAR CEO Ryan McLaughlin said the data shows "an ongoing shift in the market" in condo and townhome inventory, adding that "affordability remains a significant consideration, particularly for first-time buyers." He noted the composition of the new inventory is an important part of the story.
