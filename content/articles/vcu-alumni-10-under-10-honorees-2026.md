VCU Alumni has announced its 2026 "10 Under 10" honorees, recognizing accomplished graduates who earned their VCU degrees within the past decade. The honorees will be celebrated at an invitation-only event at the College of Health Professions.

## Leaders in business and public service

Several honorees hold leadership roles in business and organizations:

- **Rian Chandler-Dovis** (M.S. '17, Brandcenter), chief operations officer and managing partner at Richmond consultancy Brand Federation
- **Ian Kunkes** (Ed.D. '22), co-founder and CEO of EdPros, focused on higher education disability and accessibility
- **Lark Washington** (M.U.R.P. '20), chief operating officer of Homeward, the Richmond region's homeless services network
- **Nada Raafat Elkharashi** (B.F.A. '17, M.F.A. '21), designer, researcher and founder of NRK Interdisciplinary Research Design Co.

## Science, health and government

The list also includes Candice Hatcher-Solis, acting deputy director and division chief in the Air Force's space acquisition and integration office; Sana Hosseini of Aptar Pharma; Haley Mulder, chief toxicologist for the Maryland State Police; Samuel Obeng of Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center; dentist Heba Al Tamimi; and photographer and designer Riley Goodman.

Some honorees earned up to three VCU degrees, and several have returned to campus to mentor students.
