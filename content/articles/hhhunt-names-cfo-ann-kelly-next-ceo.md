HHHunt, the family-owned homebuilder and community developer based in Glen Allen, announced in early October that Chief Financial Officer Ann Kelly will become CEO on July 1, 2027, succeeding Buck Hunt, who has led the company since 2013.

## The transition

Buck Hunt, son of founder Harry H. Hunt III, will remain chairman, continue guiding long-term strategy and lead a new hospitality venture for the company. Steve Brant, now senior vice president of finance and treasury, will become CFO, and Chief Operating Officer Steve Fritz stays in his role. Kelly, Brant and Fritz will form the executive leadership team.

## Who is Ann Kelly

Kelly joined HHHunt in 2001 and spent her first decade as controller for HHHunt Homes before moving into finance leadership roles; she became CFO in 2019. She serves on the boards of the Goochland Education Foundation, GoochlandCares and Virginia Tech's Blackwood School of Real Estate.

"Ann and I are aligned on what the future holds for HHHunt," Hunt said, adding he has "nothing but confidence" in the new team. Kelly said she is "honored to be entrusted with the CEO role" and plans to build on the company's 60-year history.

HHHunt builds homes and communities in Maryland, Virginia, Tennessee, North Carolina, South Carolina and Georgia.
